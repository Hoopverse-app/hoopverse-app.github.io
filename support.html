HoopVerse Support

HoopVerse builds a basketball training plan around your week and runs each session as a timed workout. If something isn't working or you have a question, this page should help.

Contact us

Email assasinbone40@hotmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within two working days. It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot is even better.

Testing the beta?

Thanks for helping. To send feedback from TestFlight, take a screenshot in HoopVerse and tap Share Beta Feedback, or open the TestFlight app, choose HoopVerse and tap Send Beta Feedback. You can also reply to your invitation email.

Your plan

  • To change your goals, training days, session length or level, go to Profile and tap Edit goals & schedule. Your plan is rebuilt from today; past sessions and history are kept.
  • If you miss a session, the Today tab offers to move it to the best free day, pick any free day in the next two weeks, or skip it. You can do the same from the Plan tab.
  • You can pick up to five goals. Your top picks get the most sessions.
  • Days marked with a court or a partner get drills that need them. Other days only use drills you can do at home with a ball.

Workouts

  • Start today's session from the Today tab, or any workout from the Train tab. You can pause, skip forward and go back during a workout.
  • While a workout runs, a timer appears on your lock screen and in the Dynamic Island. If your phone is locked when a drill ends, open HoopVerse to carry on.
  • When a workout includes shooting, HoopVerse asks whether to track your shots. Choose Track shots to enter makes and attempts after each shooting drill.
  • Build your own workout from the drill library on the Train tab, including strength, plyometric and recovery exercises, or time a freestyle session. Search the library by name, skill or kit.
  • Strength and plyometric exercises in your own workouts use sets, reps and weight. Each set runs until you tap Set done, then a rest timer starts.
  • Give your own and freestyle workouts up to two categories, such as Strength or Conditioning, or skip it. Time that isn't tied to a drill counts towards those ratings.
  • Under Train any skill, start a ready-made workout for any skill, including ones that aren't your goals.

Your player card

The card on the Today tab rates each skill out of 99. Ratings start from your level and rise with the minutes you train each skill. Recent training counts most, so a rating slowly drifts down if you leave that skill alone. Shooting also follows the shots you log. Your goals count three times towards your overall rating. Tap the card to see the details.

Reminders and Apple Health

  • Turn training-day reminders on in Profile, under Reminders & Health, and choose the time. If they don't arrive, check that notifications for HoopVerse are allowed in the iPhone Settings app.
  • Apple Health is optional. When it's on, HoopVerse saves your workouts to Health and shows heart rate and calories in your workout summary. Heart rate and calories only appear if a watch recorded them during the workout.
  • To change Health permissions later, open the Settings app, then Privacy & Security, Health, HoopVerse.

Shot Lab

Shot Lab rates your free-throw form using your camera. Stand the phone side-on to you, far enough away that your whole body is in the frame, in good light. The camera image is analysed on your phone and is never saved or sent anywhere.

Your profile photo

Tap the photo on the Profile tab to change or remove it. It stays on your phone.

Resetting and deleting your data

  • To start again with a new plan, go to Profile and choose Reset plan and start over. This clears your plan, history, custom workouts, trophies, XP and photo.
  • To delete everything HoopVerse has stored, delete the app from your phone. Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in Health.
  • Everything you put into HoopVerse is stored only on your phone. There are no accounts, so we can't recover data from a deleted app.
