HoopVerse Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

HoopVerse is a basketball training app. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is kept, and the choices you have. In short: everything you put into HoopVerse stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

HoopVerse keeps the following only on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Your training profile: the first name and profile photo you choose to add (both optional), your level, goals, sessions per week and weekly availability.
  • Your training plan, completed sessions, workout history, custom workouts, shooting makes and attempts, trophies and XP.
  • Your settings, such as sound, reminders and Apple Health.
  • Shot Lab scores.

We do not run servers that receive this information, and we cannot see it.

Profile photo

If you add a profile photo, you choose it from your photo library or take it with the camera. HoopVerse keeps a small copy on your phone to show on your profile and player card. It is never uploaded or shared. You can change or remove it at any time from Profile. HoopVerse only sees the one photo you pick, not the rest of your library.

Camera (Shot Lab)

Shot Lab uses your camera to rate your free-throw form. The camera image is analysed on your phone by Google's ML Kit pose detection. Camera images, video and the body positions worked out from them are not recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Only the resulting scores are kept, on your phone.

ML Kit may send Google limited technical diagnostics, such as your device model, operating system version, app information, and performance and error data. Google states this data is encrypted in transit and is not shared with third parties. It does not include camera images. You can avoid this entirely by not using Shot Lab.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health in HoopVerse, the app saves your completed workouts to Health and reads your heart rate and active energy for the time of each workout, so it can show them in your workout summary and history. You choose what to allow in the iOS permission screen and can change it at any time in Settings, Privacy & Security, Health, HoopVerse.

Health information read by HoopVerse is stored only on your phone. It is never sold, never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with anyone.

Notifications

Training reminders are scheduled on your phone. No notification service or server is involved.

Beta testing through TestFlight

If you test HoopVerse through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share with us crash reports, basic device information and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. This is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy and is used only to fix problems and improve the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent information.
  • We do not show advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising services inside the app.

Children

HoopVerse does not ask for or collect personal information that leaves the device, and it has no accounts, chat or social features. Parents and guardians who have questions can contact us using the details below.

Deleting your information

  • To delete everything HoopVerse has stored, delete the app from your phone.
  • To remove your profile photo, tap it on the Profile tab and choose Remove photo.
  • To clear your plan, workout history, custom workouts, trophies, XP and profile photo but keep using the app, go to Profile and choose Reset plan and start over. Shot Lab scores are kept by this option.
  • Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay in Health until you delete them there.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted in the app and at the same web address, with a new effective date. Significant changes will be highlighted in the app.
