HoopVerse Privacy Policy

Effective 28 November 2026

HoopVerse is a basketball training app. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is kept, and the choices you have. In short: if you don't make an account, everything you put into HoopVerse stays on your phone. An account is optional. If you make one, we keep a backup of your training and the little that friends and leagues need, and nothing more. There is no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Without an account, HoopVerse keeps the following only on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Your training profile: the first name and profile photo you choose to add (both optional), your level, goals, sessions per week and weekly availability.
  • Your training plan, completed sessions, workout history, custom workouts, shooting makes and attempts, sets, reps and weights, trophies and XP.
  • Your settings, such as sound, reminders and Apple Health.
  • Shot Lab scores.

Some of this is never sent anywhere, even with an account: your settings, your training plan (it is rebuilt from your profile), Shot Lab scores, and anything read from Apple Health.

Accounts (optional)

You can use all of HoopVerse's training features without an account. If you choose to make one, you sign in with Apple. We don't ask Apple for your name or email address; Apple gives us an anonymous identifier for your account, and we use it only to recognise you when you sign in.

When you make an account, you choose:

  • A username and a display name. Both are checked against a list of words that aren't allowed.
  • Whether friends and leagues can see you.

You also enter your date of birth. We use it only to work out an age range (13 to 15, 16 to 17, or adult) and keep that range, not the date. You must be 13 or older to make an account.

What your account keeps

If you have an account, our server keeps:

  • Your account identifier from Apple, your username, display name, avatar choice, age range and friends setting.
  • A backup of your training profile, workouts and custom workouts, so you can restore them on a new phone. This includes your first name and profile photo if you added them. They are part of your backup only, and other players never see them.
  • For each workout from the last eight days, when it started, how long it lasted and what kind it was, so the server can work out your XP for leaderboards.
  • How much each of your ratings rose this week.
  • Your friends, friend requests, invite links you created, players you have blocked, and any reports you make.

Apple Health readings, such as heart rate and active energy, are never included in backups. They stay on your phone and in Apple Health.

Friends and leagues

If you turn on friends, your friends and the players in your weekly league can see your display name, avatar choice, level, weekly XP, sessions this week and how much your ratings rose this week. They cannot see your workouts, your ratings themselves, your photo, your first name, your shots or any health information.

  • You add friends by sharing an invite link or code, or, if you are 18 or over, by username. Players under 18 can only add friends with an invite link or code.
  • Players under 18 are only placed in leagues with other players under 18.
  • You can remove or block anyone at any time. Blocking hides you from each other everywhere in HoopVerse.
  • If you report a player, we keep the report and your reason so we can review it. The player is not told who reported them.

You can turn friends off at any time in Profile. Your backup keeps working.

Invite links open our website, which is hosted by GitHub Pages. If HoopVerse isn't installed, the page shows the invite code. GitHub may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, when the page is visited.

Where account information is kept

Account information is stored by Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, on servers in the European Union (Ireland). Supabase processes it only on our instructions. Sign in with Apple is provided by Apple under its own privacy policy.

We use your account information only to provide backup, friends and leagues. Our legal basis for doing so is that you asked for these features by making an account.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, everything about you is deleted from our database straight away. Any copies in our provider's routine backups are removed within 30 days. Reports you made about other players are kept without your name.

Profile photo

If you add a profile photo, you choose it from your photo library or take it with the camera. HoopVerse keeps a small copy on your phone to show on your profile and player card. If you have an account, it is also part of your private backup. It is never shown to other players. You can change or remove it at any time from Profile. HoopVerse only sees the one photo you pick, not the rest of your library.

Camera (Shot Lab)

Shot Lab uses your camera to rate your free-throw form. The camera image is analysed on your phone by Google's ML Kit pose detection. Camera images, video and the body positions worked out from them are not recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Only the resulting scores are kept, on your phone.

ML Kit may send Google limited technical diagnostics, such as your device model, operating system version, app information, and performance and error data. Google states this data is encrypted in transit and is not shared with third parties. It does not include camera images. You can avoid this entirely by not using Shot Lab.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health in HoopVerse, the app saves your completed workouts to Health and reads your heart rate and active energy for the time of each workout, so it can show them in your workout summary and history. You choose what to allow in the iOS permission screen and can change it at any time in Settings, Privacy & Security, Health, HoopVerse.

Health information read by HoopVerse is stored only on your phone. It is never uploaded, never sold, never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with anyone, even if you have an account.

Notifications

Training reminders are scheduled on your phone. No notification service or server is involved.

Beta testing through TestFlight

If you test HoopVerse through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share with us crash reports, basic device information and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. This is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy and is used only to fix problems and improve the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent information.
  • We do not show advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising services inside the app.

Children

HoopVerse's training features can be used by anyone, with nothing leaving the device. Accounts are only for people aged 13 or over. For players aged 13 to 17, friends are off to start with, friends can only be added with an invite link or code, and leagues only include other players under 18. Parents and guardians who have questions can contact us using the details below.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the information your account holds, ask us to correct it, or delete it. Deleting your account in the app removes everything. To ask for a copy, email us from the address below and tell us your username.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Deleting your information

  • To delete your account and everything our server holds about you, go to Profile, then Account, and choose Delete account. Your training stays on your phone.
  • To delete everything HoopVerse has stored on your phone, delete the app.
  • To remove your profile photo, tap it on the Profile tab and choose Remove photo.
  • To clear your plan, workout history, custom workouts, trophies, XP and profile photo but keep using the app, go to Profile and choose Reset plan and start over. Shot Lab scores are kept by this option.
  • Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay in Health until you delete them there.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted in the app and at the same web address, with a new effective date. Significant changes will be highlighted in the app.
